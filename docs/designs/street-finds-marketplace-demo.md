## Problem Statement
Valuable items get thrown away on the street every day. Existing channels (Facebook Marketplace free section, Craigslist curb alerts, Trash Nothing) stop at "someone posted junk." Nobody shows what happens next — the refurbishment, the transformation, the resale. best-of-waste is a three-persona marketplace: **Scouts** find curbside items and list them, **Flippers** claim and restore them, **Buyers** purchase the restored goods with a visible transformation story.

## What Makes This Cool
- Snap-to-list: photograph a curbside item, AI identifies and lists it in seconds.
- Treasure map: a city view of available street finds.
- Transformation timeline: before photo → restoration notes → after photo. This is the "whoa" — provenance is what no competitor shows and the reason a buyer pays flipper prices instead of free.

## Constraints
- Deadline: tonight. Solo builder.
- Stack: Next.js (App Router) + Vercel + Neon (Postgres), Tailwind, one Anthropic vision call for snap-to-list. Photo storage: Vercel Blob (or Supabase if bundling DB+storage).
- Demo shown on a phone browser (responsive web, no mobile app tonight).

## Premises
1. The demo spine is ONE item's scripted journey: scout snaps → flipper claims → timeline fills → sold. All three features appear as one story.
2. Realism tonight = seeded data. Persona-switch buttons instead of auth; seeded map pins; only snap-to-list is live. *(Revised after second opinion: the map is styled as a decorative neighborhood-activity view, not a claim of real-time listings — a visibly decorative map beats a visibly fake live map.)*
3. Responsive web demoed on a phone beats building mobile tonight.
4. The differentiator vs FB Marketplace/Craigslist/Trash Nothing is the flipper middle layer — the transformation story.

## Cross-Model Perspective

Build the state machine well; all three personas are just views over it. Concretely: an `item_events` table that IS the timeline — one query renders scout snap, flipper notes, and the sale.

## Approaches Considered
- **A: Scripted Journey (minimal viable)** — one app, three tabs, one item's journey, seeded elsewhere. Effort S, risk Low. Chosen.
- **B: Two Live Personas** — real DB writes for scout + flipper flows. Effort M, risk Medium (2x state management at 1am). Ruled out: demo risk if a write path breaks live.
- **C: Transformation-First** — the app IS the timeline; invert the demo. Effort S-M, risk Low. Not chosen as spine; its before/after slider is stolen into A as the timeline centerpiece.

## Recommended Approach
**A + C's before/after slider.** One item's journey is a spine judges can follow; the slider is the emotional beat; snap-to-list is the single live whoa moment. Map is decorative activity view with the live item pulsing. Three screens: **Map / Item detail / List** (the snap-to-list flow lives on the List screen). **Live-vs-seeded boundary:** live DB writes are the snap-to-list insert, the flipper claim, and restoration notes; everything else (map pins, other items, the hero item's pre-snap history) is pre-seeded.

Schema (from second opinion, adopted):
- `items(id, title, description, ai_guess, status[found|claimed|restored|sold], lat, lng, price, claimer_id, created_at)` — `claimer_id` references seeded persona constants (fixed ids in the seed script: `scout-1`, `flipper-1`, `buyer-1`); the persona-switch cookie's id populates it on claim. `lat/lng` for the live item: hardcoded demo-neighborhood coordinates (no geolocation tonight). Status + claimer_id are updated in the same write as each new event (single source of truth).
- `item_events(id, item_id, type, photo_url, note, created_at)` — the events table IS the timeline. `type ∈ {found_snap, claim, restoration_note, sold}`.

Demo spine mechanics (from outside voice):
- The hero item claimed LIVE is distinct from the item carrying the seeded before/after slider. The live-claimed item ends its demo arc at the "Mark sold" button press; the seeded slider item is shown first as the emotional opener.
- `sold` write: a "Mark sold" action (flipper persona) sets status + writes the `sold` event — this closes the journey live.
- After photo source: the slider item's after photo is seeded (shot/stocked before the demo); the restoration-note form accepts a photo but the live item won't use it mid-demo.
